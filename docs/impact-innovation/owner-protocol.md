# Owner research protocol and consent record

Prepared 6 November 2026. Confirmed participants: **0** (Fouad's explicit update). Desired count: 3; no booking or outcome is assumed. Use identifiers P01–P03 in the repository. Store names, contact details, signed consent and private documents outside source control with access limited to the team and participant permission.

## Before showing Raysto

Confirm independent café/snack-bar ownership or responsibility for purchases/menu pricing. Record venue type, role, relationship to Fouad and potential acquaintance bias. Explain voluntary participation, stopping/withdrawing, the sample nature of tasks and that declining a recommendation is a valid result.

Record separate choices for participation, audio/video recording, anonymous quotations, attributed quotations and review of private business evidence. A yes to participation is not consent to recording or publication. Agree retention/deletion expectations. Do not collect customer, employee or supplier identity data unnecessarily.

Ask without leading:

1. Tell me about the last supplier price increase you noticed.
2. How did you work out which dishes it affected? Show or describe the records and tools.
3. What did you do next, and what influenced that decision?
4. What worked well in your current method? What, if anything, was difficult?
5. What would make a price recommendation unacceptable?
6. What effort would you tolerate to set up and maintain ingredient/recipe records?

Record concrete events, contradictions and cases where the problem does not arise. Do not replace absent pain with a hypothetical affirmative answer.

## Matched task procedure

Use [the two input cards](exercises.md). Give identical underlying evidence to the usual-method and Raysto conditions. The usual method may be a spreadsheet, paper/calculator or the participant's available software. Do not deliberately withhold useful records from it. If no normal method exists, record the baseline chosen and its limitation.

Counterbalance in advance: P01 usual method A then Raysto B; P02 Raysto A then usual method B; P03 usual method B then Raysto A. With only three people the design is not fully balanced; report that limitation. Do not switch the order after seeing performance. Record deviations and technical interruptions.

Measure active setup separately from recurring investigation. Raysto checkpoint timing is elapsed wall time including pauses; it is not an automatic active-work metric. Start the recurring timer only when confirmed inputs are present and the new increase task is introduced. Record a fresh setup task separately; never count a preconfigured fixture as zero-effort onboarding.

Observe the six tasks in the agreed plan. Let the participant describe contribution, assumed volume, break-even and proposal/execution in their own words. Keep requests neutral (“What are you thinking?”). Record every task-specific prompt as assistance. If they abandon or make an incorrect decision, preserve it. Stop for discomfort or request; include the unfinished task in the denominator with a reason.

After both conditions, ask what they would keep, change or decline, expected maintenance burden, confidence in evidence, and what would prevent use. Optional real-document discussion requires separate consent and original currency. Never relabel MAD/EUR records as USD or insert them into the judge café. This discussion tests relevance/mapping, not financial improvement.

## Recording and reporting

Use [owner-observations.csv](owner-observations.csv), one row per person/condition, with evidence notes stored under the anonymous ID. Record setup seconds, recurring seconds, each task's correctness, comprehension, prompts, corrections, decision/reason, abandonment and technical failure. Retain raw observations and any corrected coding separately.

Report individual results and exact denominators. Compute paired recurring-time reduction only where both methods completed with comparable evidence and accuracy. Show excluded/abandoned pairs and why. The internal targets are 2/3 unassisted, correct amounts for every completed decision, 2/3 understanding, an exploratory 30% median time reduction and 15-minute setup. Do not report these as achieved until observed.

One or two owners yield case studies. Zero owners yields internal usability evidence only. No statistical significance, causal profit improvement, predicted demand effect or invented quotation is permitted.

## Internal AI contribution comparison

Run the same fixed tasks with independent reference arithmetic, Raysto structured controls and the assistant. Record time/accuracy/help separately. The calculation engine deserves credit for exact numbers; only measured conversational or analytical assistance belongs to AI. Keep actual model failures, refusals, fallback frequency and latency. A scripted tool test does not enter the actual-model success denominator.
