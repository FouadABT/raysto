# Preregistered owner hypotheses

6 November 2026, before any owner session. These hypotheses were agreed before implementation; this register was written while engineering implementation was underway. Internal fixture testing has occurred. It is not owner research.

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence to collect | Contradictory finding | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Owners struggle to trace supplier increases into dish costs. | Concrete most-recent event, tools, missing records, time, mistakes. | Owner already solves it quickly and reliably, or does not need dish-level tracing. | Record the counterexample; narrow audience/problem rather than reinterpret satisfaction as pain. |
| H2 | Connected evidence reduces recurring investigation effort. | Matched task time, accuracy, assistance, failures in both methods. | No time reduction, lower accuracy, or more help needed. | Fix friction or retain the simpler method. Include setup effort when considering net benefit. |
| H3 | Owners need to challenge recommendations to trust a decision. | Unprompted objections, changes to assumptions, decision reason, comprehension. | Owners prefer fixed controls or find conversation slower/confusing. | Keep manual controls primary and make AI optional; do not attribute software benefits to AI. |
| H4 | One dish can give useful value without full-business setup. | Active setup time, missing units/costs, maintenance burden, incomplete-dish refusal. | Setup exceeds tolerated effort, requires unavailable evidence, or benefit is too small. | Improve mappings and progressive setup; do not hide missing inputs or preconfigure privately. |

Internal targets: at least 2/3 owners finish unaided; all completed decisions use correct supplied amounts; at least 2/3 distinguish assumptions; explore 30% lower median recurring-task time without lower accuracy and setup within 15 minutes. Targets are not results. Report each person and exact denominators, including abandonment. Three sessions cannot establish population effects or statistical significance.

Findings register: **no owner observations yet**. Keep contrary and favorable findings together; append dates, anonymized participant IDs and evidence references. Never replace an original hypothesis after seeing results without recording the change.
