# Raysto — research and creative brief for the 38-second judging segment

Research date: 27 October 2026. This segment follows the founder's own 10-second introduction. It explains the technical proposition before the longer working demonstration. The interpretation of successful examples below is editorial judgment, not evidence that a particular video technique caused a win.

## What this competition actually rewards

PayPal requires meaningful PayPal and AI integration, an accessible working project, public licensed source and a public YouTube demonstration under three minutes. The video should include the application functioning on its intended device. Animated explanation can establish context; it cannot replace that evidence. [Official rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules)

The five criteria are technological implementation, design, potential impact, innovation and presentation. The technical criterion asks for non-trivial working integration; the presentation criterion asks for a clear end-to-end demonstration. The current panel includes PayPal developers, engineers and a product manager, plus sponsor representatives. We should explain the business use case and expose the critical mechanisms without assuming a panelist's private preferences. [Criteria and panel](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/)

AG Grid's sponsor material emphasizes an integrated workspace and AG Studio capabilities. The segment uses the actual connected management view, while the later demo should demonstrate its query and selection behavior. [Sponsor brief](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/aggrid)

## Advice from judges and experienced entrants

Devpost's judge interviews describe looking at working functionality, requirements, originality and coherent storytelling across the video and written submission. This supports a clear explanation followed by inspectable behavior; visual polish alone is insufficient. [Judge interviews](https://info.devpost.com/blog/hackathon-judging-tips)

Devpost's participant advice recommends scripting, introducing the purpose quickly, showing the application responding to input, and avoiding an overcrowded or artificially sped-up pitch. [Demo-video guidance](https://info.devpost.com/blog/6-tips-for-making-a-hackathon-demo-video)

YC's presentation advice reinforces stating what the product does early and keeping each visual easy to understand. Its investor demo-day context differs from a hackathon: its caution about live demos does not override this competition's explicit requirement to demonstrate working software. [YC presentation guide](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/)

## The judge-facing proposition

**Raysto coordinates restaurant commerce across diners, staff and AI, with shared state and individually approved PayPal payments.**

The memorable mechanism is one group request becoming a priced proposal, a host-approved order, exact bill allocations and a PayPal handoff. Gemini proposes; deterministic server logic calculates and authorizes; people approve; the provider supplies financial evidence. PayPal MCP is a read-only status investigation path, separate from guarded payment mutations. Staff see the same visit in the AG Grid / AG Studio workspace.

Do not spend this segment listing reservations, delivery, subscriptions, every API or every sponsor. Those capabilities dilute the central journey and can be documented separately. Do not invent traction, time savings, restaurant testimonials or successful captures.

## Reference-derived visual direction

The supplied Weepo reference was inspected across its 38-second timeline at 0.2-second intervals. Its useful techniques are textured paper, monochrome people, imperfect silhouettes, contrasting headline/handwritten/document typography, drawn annotations and objects moving along explanatory paths. Longer compositions evolve through staggered reveals. The soundtrack was not audited by listening.

For Raysto: warm paper, forest and lime; original illustrative photographic artwork; menu slips, kitchen tickets and bill fragments; ink arrows and approval stamps. Every transition moves a meaningful object into its next role. No reference footage, script, brand assets or music is reused.

## Evidence boundaries

The $27 basket, recorded Gemini tools, submitted order, individual share proposals and manager answer are historical QA evidence. The photo is AI-generated illustrative artwork, not pilot footage. Native footage is from the Android emulator. PayPal handoff is genuine Sandbox; buyer capture is not verified. Direct official MCP reads passed; complete Gemini-to-MCP chat acceptance remains pending. Google capacity and the remaining payment/device gates are recorded in `../docs/project-status.md`.

The rendered film is an explanation with labelled recorded evidence. The remaining approximately two minutes of the final submission should show current functioning behavior, exact provider results and at least one meaningful recovery/control case. Avoid making the whole submission an animation.
